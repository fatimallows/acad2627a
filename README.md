## 1. Purpose & Role Summary

The Academic Affairs Committee is responsible for:
- Looking after the academic improvement of the members of the organization.
- Arranging seminars and the like, on topics relevant and significant to the BS CS curriculum
- Initiating projects, such as tutorials, for the academic welfare of the members
- Caring, managing, and promulgating the Organization's physical and online library; and
- Keeping and updating copies of examinations, lectures, and machine problems.
